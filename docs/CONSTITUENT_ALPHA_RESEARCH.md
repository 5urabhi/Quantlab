# BANKNIFTY underlying-first alpha research

## Objective

Study 5–60 minute conditional index movements, not bid/ask scalping or latency arbitrage. Predict the underlying before choosing instruments. This feature is research-only and does not modify the existing automatic paper selector or send orders.

## Open and prepare

Open **Market Intelligence → Alpha Research**. The existing Data setup section enables cash-equity capture, loads today's official membership, and imports reviewed dated weights; Signal research enables the existing prospective paired decision ledger. These remain explicit user actions. Quotes, index ticks, membership evidence and weights use the existing canonical library. Do not change recorder subscriptions or restart during capture just to run the study.

Alpha Research reports capture/ledger consent and completed recorded sessions. **Audit evidence & run model comparison** works even without sufficient training data and exports the coverage audit CSV. No historical backfilled calls or current weights are inserted into the prospective ledger.

## Frozen research specification

- Existing prospective protocol: 5, 10, 30 and 60-minute recorded outcomes. The 15-minute label requires a new prospective protocol version; it is not fabricated or silently substituted.
- Index features: 5/15/30-minute changes from earlier receipt-valid reference observations, observed one-minute realized volatility (at least 20 adjacent returns), EMA9/21 gap, RSI14 and time of session. No missing price is forward-filled.
- Constituent features: contemporaneous reviewed weights and aligned five-minute constituent returns, breadth, weighted dispersion, signed relative volume (five prior same-time samples required per constituent), ATP deviation and displayed buy/sell quantity imbalance. Optional aggregate features use at least 80% observed weight and a separate missingness flag. ATP is not necessarily a verified exchange VWAP, and displayed quantity is not aggressor flow.
- Membership and weight provenance must be present. Each included constituent current/anchor receipt must be available at its corresponding cutoff. Full weighted coverage is required for the matched sample; no equal-weight fallback.
- Split the first 60 completed **recorded** sessions in the original protocol into 30 training, 10 calibration, 20 holdout. Unsupported days are retained, not replaced. Current session is excluded. Require at least 60/20/40 scored samples from at least 20/7/12 supported sessions and 10 training samples in each class. More history is desirable before real decisions.
- Select non-overlapping decision windows greedily, including the 30-second target receipt allowance. A missing outcome reserves its window and remains counted. Samples can still be serially dependent.
- Three classes: future index return less than −5 bps, between −5 and +5 inclusive, above +5. Models: index-only standardized L2 logistic; matched constituent logistic; matched constituent histogram gradient boosting. Fixed classifier settings in code; calibration temperature chosen on the 10 calibration sessions, never holdout.
- Development expanding-window checks: first 20→next 5, first 25→next 5 sessions, subject to support. Final model assessment uses the frozen train/calibration split on later 20 sessions. This is a local chronological assessment, not an external preregistration or evidence that the researcher never saw those dates.
- Baselines: training class prior, no trade, 30-minute index momentum, rule-based regime momentum, EMA/RSI, weighted pressure and volume-confirmed pressure. The rule-based regime uses 30-minute movement relative to observed one-minute volatility; compression uses a 3 bps one-minute volatility threshold. It is not a fitted HMM.
- Class probabilities, Brier and log loss, classification accuracy, confidence reliability, selective direction hits, call coverage and descriptive net proxy are reported on matched observations. Probability models abstain unless the highest class is directional and has probability at least 0.60. The fixed hypothetical 5 bps round-trip index cost proxy is not an option fee, a spot fill or actual money.
- Conditional 10/50/90th percentile return regressors use training-only gradient boosting, with pinball losses against unconditional training quantiles, interval coverage and quantile crossing diagnostics. No future quote is used as a feature.
- Descriptive model gate: Brier better than training prior and, for constituent models, better than index-only logistic; at least 20 calls over 10 sessions; positive mean signed net index proxy. No gate is proof of significance, option profitability, or permission to trade.

- Option context uses the closest expiry with a saved, hash-checked BANKNIFTY chain received within 90 seconds of the decision cutoff, at least five paired master-verified strikes within ±3% of spot, and positive aggregate OI on each side. Put/call OI and volume, concentration, ATM IV, approximate 1% OTM put-call skew, days to expiry and 15-minute changes are descriptive features. Import records and raw reports are retained in the same shared SQLite store, with SHA-256 provenance. No provider credentials or separate database are needed.
- Receipt mode requires the actual import time as well as source availability and excludes archive-mode rows. Archive mode explicitly trusts user-reviewed historical availability timestamps; it cannot establish independently verified prospective availability. One consistent source identity per factor is required. Revisions become visible only at their availability time. The source audit remains available even if model fitting is blocked.

## Historical acquisition and retrospective alpha research

Under **Market Intelligence → Alpha Research → Historical Alpha Research**, use the existing resumable Dhan batch downloader for `banknifty_pit`, one-minute bars, and the original index spot. Select up to 240 calendar days with an end-exclusive date no later than the current IST date. Historical membership must already be synchronized and the Dhan catalog/token available. The user explicitly authorizes data requests and acknowledges unresolved historical constituents. A batch reuses existing canonical candle checkpoints; it is not a new independent market database. Use Data Center's existing resume/cancel controls for interrupted downloads.

The historical research job reads SHA-checked original BANKNIFTY spot candles from that exact batch and exact NSE-equity identities from the pinned membership intervals. It does not use a current membership list to backfill past days. A current broker master can fail to resolve historical/delisted identities; these gaps are reported and complete-member windows are required. This archive is a third-party reconstruction, not exchange certification or proof of the information available at a historical instant.

- Research supports 5/10/15/30/60 minutes and session-close returns from completed bar closes. The first 60 observed index dates use 30/10/20 train/calibration/holdout sessions. Session-close uses 120 dates (60/20/40) because there is only one reserved forecast window per date. The first dates are retained even when coverage is poor. Missing labels do not shift the fixed anchor grid.
- Features include 5/15/30-minute index returns, EMA and RSI, realized and EWMA volatility, a finite rolling directional-movement (ADX-like) proxy, opening range, constituent return/breadth/dispersion and past-only relative volume. Research compares index-only and constituent models plus opening-range breakout, pullback and range-reversion rules.
- Unweighted research uses arithmetic constituent breadth and returns as features only, not official index weights or contributions. Verified-weight mode requires matching dated snapshots actually recorded before the session. Historical weights without independently supported availability remain unavailable in this mode.
- This is latest-retrieved retrospective OHLCV research. It does not write reconstructed calls or fills to the live prospective ledger. Close-to-close labels are predictions, not executable index or options returns. The existing hypothetical 5 bps index proxy, matched comparisons, calibration and support gates apply. Audit CSV includes the membership/source hashes, missing source reasons and date-by-date coverage; it exists even when models cannot train.

## Volatility evidence research

Market Intelligence → Alpha Research → Volatility Research contains two independent studies. Same-contract IV changes use original SHA-checked BANKNIFTY option chain snapshots, the nearest paired ATM strike selected at the entry cutoff, identical call/put identities and expiry at the later cutoff, and reserved non-overlapping windows with a two-minute gap. IV values are vendor percentages; differences are percentage points. Receipt windows are bounded, and the actual elapsed time and both snapshot hashes are saved. All-sample quantiles are descriptive. The first 20 observed dates estimate quantiles and the following 10 assess the training 90% interval and the fixed ±2 percentage-point stress; minimum sample/day support is required. No estimate is installed automatically in the paper engine.

Realized-volatility research reads original index spot candles from the selected historical batch. Adjacent one-minute log returns are never constructed across missing bars or overnight. Fixed horizons compare unconditional training variance, trailing 30-minute variance, EWMA (0.94), a training-only regularized intraday HAR-style 5/15/30-minute variance regression, and stationary GARCH(1,1) Gaussian quasi-likelihood. GARCH resets at separate observed segments and reports optimizer failure as unavailable. The target is mean squared future adjacent log returns in bps², not annualized IV; MAE of volatility, RMSE of variance and QLIKE are reported for calibration and holdout periods. First 60 observed dates split 30/10/20, with matched samples and minimum support. No fees, option P&L or fill claims are inferred from these forecasts. CSV evidence remains available if fitting is blocked.

Tests: `python -m unittest discover -s tests -p 'test_volatility_evidence.py' -v`. The earlier 2pp stress is retained in the existing paper selector until a separately validated policy can use appropriate empirical conditional distributions. Additional fitted regimes, LightGBM/XGBoost, distribution-driven instrument evaluation and prospective performance remain distinct work.


## Fitted regime and additional classifier comparisons

Historical alpha v2 adds causal standardized return-shift CUSUM features and train-only three-state diagonal Gaussian mixture and Gaussian HMM comparisons. GMM initialization, 15 bounded HMM EM steps, variance floors and smoothed transitions use only training observations. HMM sequences reset at session boundaries and missing fixed-grid windows. Calibration and holdout use filtered state probabilities (no backward smoothing/Viterbi hindsight) as extra logistic-regression features. Session-close windows or inadequate contiguous transitions leave HMM unavailable. States are statistical clusters, not assumed bullish/bearish labels.

Optional LightGBM and XGBoost CPU classifiers use fixed shallow models, training-only fitting and calibration-only temperature selection on the same holdout samples as index and constituent logistic regression. Install explicitly with `.venv/bin/pip install -r requirements-alpha-optional.txt`; missing dependencies or model fit failure are reported, never substituted with a different model. No tuning is performed against the test dates, and multiple model comparisons remain exploratory. Tests: `python -m unittest discover -s tests -p 'test_fitted_alpha.py' -v`. Synthetic tests and optional dependency detection are not empirical alpha evidence. Distribution-driven option execution and independent future model validation remain unimplemented.

## Prediction-to-option research and frozen candidates

Open **Market Intelligence → Alpha Research → Predictive Option Research**. Two independently labeled evidence modes are available. Both compare fixed chronological windows, use a 30/10/20 split for the five intraday horizons and keep model training/calibration separate from later outcomes. Neither makes any provider requests or broker orders.

### Historical rolling-candle proxy

Choose a saved dated-membership batch (automatic market-library maintenance creates these). The study reads hash-checked shared index and bank candles plus existing saved near-monthly ATM ±3 rolling-option receipts. The index/constituent classifiers learn from training sessions and temperature-calibrate only on calibration sessions. Empirical earlier-session option-response analogs match option structure, class, moneyness, premium relative to spot and time of day. Same-family paired-leg changes come from one earlier observed window, not independently sampled legs. Each class requires at least eight distinct training anchors across at least five sessions, bounded nearest analogs and a fixed distance limit. Class probabilities weight these scenarios. No forecast can use the later option entry/exit bars to choose its contract. Compare index and constituent logistic/boosting, directional-only and debit-spread-only ablations, EMA-RSI, pressure, breakout, pullback, range reversion and no trade.

Model long CE/PE and debit verticals only. Select a same-session strike/side/monthly family at the completed signal close, model entry at the open one full minute after the decision, exit at the open at the fixed target. Positive bar volume is evidence of activity, not fill proof. Exact historical security IDs, historical exchange lot sizes and bid/ask/depth are unavailable in these rolling receipts; quantity is an explicit hypothetical historical lot size. Gross buy plus estimated roundtrip fees is a conservative funding proxy, not certified broker margin or a guaranteed loss ceiling. Default capital ₹17,000, modeled loss allowance ₹4,250, maximum 3 lots, adverse premium penalty 25 bps per leg and additional 10 bps sensitivity. These are editable research assumptions, not empirically validated optimal costs. Require positive modeled net under both cost scenarios and at least 55% modeled stress profitability; otherwise no trade. Each method has a separate hypothetical account. Missing entry or exit evidence halts its session, and conditional cash/P&L omits unresolved outcomes. Current brokerage tariff is a historical counterfactual. Do not sum independent account results or call them verified trading profits.

### Original-depth forecast replay

Select the prospective ledger + original depth evidence mode. Requires 60 completed recorded sessions with matched constituent features and subsequent labels. Class-conditional earlier-return quantiles and calibrated model probabilities generate discrete BSM spot/IV scenarios using exact contract terms, with declared fixed ±2 percentage-point IV stresses. These are not calibrated joint distributions. A private historical-only source screen retains chain hash, original depth, contract master, freshness, activity, implied-volatility and leg-skew gates; it does not require the EMA trend to be directional. Selection considers long options and debit verticals, optionally credit verticals only with a conspicuous unverified-margin warning. Size from verified lot size, modeled funding/risk and displayed liquidity. The existing paper entry/exit functions replay the first eligible original receipt after a 1-second assumed reaction delay within 5 seconds and exit at the frozen target clock. Missing and partial baskets censor the session; no actual broker margin, queue position, or fill certification. Existing live paper selector is unchanged.

### New prospective horizons

The original 1/5/10/30/60-minute rule ledger and its source policy hash are unchanged. An additive source protocol records 15-minute and near-session-close labels. Near-close target is the first original qualified index tick between 15:29:20 and 15:29:50 IST, not official closing or settlement price; only record decisions at least five minutes before target. Alpha studies select non-overlapping windows; near-close needs 120 recorded dates (60/20/40) because there is at most one independent selected window per session. The new horizon policies and forward context cohort IDs remain separate from original horizons. No backfilled signals.

### Candidate registry

Successful predictive option training automatically stores supported logistic candidates as checksum-addressed portable JSON containing scaler and coefficients, calibration temperature, source policy, distinct training/calibration/reference-holdout dates and exact feature/label fingerprints. Portable predictions are checked against the original fitted estimator. No pickle or live approval. Historical versus prospective feature schemas are not interchangeable. The Alpha Research model registry allows receipt-based candidate monitoring on the first 20 recorded sessions strictly after the original holdout. Reuse the frozen coefficients and report Brier comparison; a deterioration greater than 0.05 is a descriptive flag, not a statistical guarantee. Missing support blocks the comparison. Automatic periodic retraining, independently verified empirical IV calibration, prediction-driven adaptive exit policies in live paper, verified broker margin and final prospective performance remain outstanding. No automatic model promotion or order execution.
