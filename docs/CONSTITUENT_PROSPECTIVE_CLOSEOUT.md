# Constituent research closeout: protocol, provenance and local acceptance

## Scope
- Frozen prospective weighted-price pressure and volume-confirmed pressure rules, compared with the existing EMA9/21 + RSI baseline. Independent local worker, explicit opt-in, no new provider request, no orders, no replay masquerading as a prospective decision. Original tick receipt cutoffs reconstruct completed EMA bars. One real decision clock, five target horizons; target labels require the first original same-session index tick whose exchange and receipt clocks are in the next 30 seconds after the target. Missing evidence and abstention remain distinct.
- The same canonical SQLite database stores the derived paired decision ledger, packet hashes, reviewed weight and membership hashes and future label evidence. Dashboard reports individual coverage, matched directional accuracy, confusion counts, and descriptive session bootstrap intervals (20 matched dates / 100 calls minimum). Not a profitability claim.
- Probability assessment: first 30 ended recorded IST dates for empirical estimates, next 20 untouched recorded dates for evaluation, with no substitute dates and no parameter tuning. At least 100 training and 50 assessment calls per direction plus 15/10 supported dates. A better held-out Brier score than the training base rate and per-group absolute error <=10pp are required to show an accepted conditional estimate. Actual observed outcomes are still needed. Estimates are conditional on calls with nonflat verifiable outcomes, not unconditional forecasts or guarantees. No model fitting beyond empirical frequencies, no live trade recommendations.
- Reviewed source weight ingestion can normalize a selected original local NSE intake CSV and check its preserved SHA-256. Exact dated membership, explicit percentage units/date review and complete weights remain necessary; no direct official website automation or unlicensed scraping.
- Reviewed calendar audit uses explicit OPEN/CLOSED dates and a separately affirmed exact-source/instrument tracking expectation. Bounded canonical SQL counts in IST; distinguishes missing expected completed session, closed, observed, unfinished, and unverified. At least one record is not proof of full intraday coverage.
- Conditional signed gamma scenarios use same-receipt model gamma and reported OI with explicit hypothetical fractions and units. Underlying-units or contracts x verified lot size, sign assumptions required. No actual dealer position or hedge inference.
- Optional stock derivatives: exact local master FUTSTK/OPTSTK underlying symbol/ID mapping, automatic unique nearest futures and at most 50 explicitly selected stock options. All existing option/index/cash subscriptions retain priority; only spare socket capacity. No sixth connection or forced restart. Optional plan errors fail closed; immediate rejected mixed subscriptions fall back without stock derivatives. Separate queue, sampled quote/OI storage and source provenance; stale/missing values excluded. Compact master without explicit underlying columns is blocked, not guessed.
