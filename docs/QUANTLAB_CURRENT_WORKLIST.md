## BANKNIFTY mathematical premium-target research (no ML/RL)

`bash run.sh banknifty-math --sessions 90 --capital 17000 --budget 12000 --lot-size 30 --max-lots 3 --wings 5` tests long BANKNIFTY CE/PE only. The 10% target and 5% stop are net returns relative to adverse-adjusted paid premium, after modeled fees. The budget caps full premium funding and cumulative realized losses, not a guaranteed stop-loss.

The first mathematical model uses earlier training-session BANKNIFTY same-clock price paths, conditions on 15-minute momentum direction when sufficiently supported, rescales paths using past 30-minute RMS returns, and inverts the signal premium under a zero-carry Black-Scholes proxy. Reprice under constant IV and nominal monthly expiry, then calculate modeled realized target probability, stop probability and net returns with one-minute execution delay and 35/75 bps adverse premium costs per side. Gate on positive expected net and at least 35% modeled realized target probability under both cost scenarios. No future option price participates in contract selection. Reuse the verified saved rolling option candles and existing fixed-target/stop replay with exact modeled fee calculation, mandatory 15:20 exit, no overlapping positions, at most three attempts/day, and conservative execution-gap stress accounting. Choose among 5/10/15/30/60-minute horizons on validation only; later historical assessment only for a qualifying policy. A no-trade result is legitimate.

This is exploratory, not validated or production trading. The assumed lot size, nominal expiry, constant IV, zero carry, lack of historical bid/ask and original execution latency are explicit limitations. Previously examined evaluation dates are not a fresh holdout. Other indices and constituent/global feeds are reserved for later BANKNIFTY-only context ablations, not additional trading targets. No provider calls, orders, or recorder restart. Use `--shared-root /path/to/quantlab` from an isolated checkout to read existing shared data and write new result JSON to the original results folder.

## BANKNIFTY mathematical research: user-selected flat INR execution costs

## BANKNIFTY rolling five-minute OHLC forecast v1

`bash run.sh banknifty-ohlc --days 365 --wings 3` reads the existing verified local source receipts and produces JSON research metrics plus one CSV of the selected model's out-of-sample predictions and actual OHLC. This is one index-forecasting task, not option trade selection. Every complete decision minute forecasts the next five full minutes, with the previous 60 contiguous minutes as input. OHLC is encoded relative to the last known close with nonnegative wick components and reconstructed with valid ordering. Includes raw 60-minute BANKNIFTY OHLC sequence, past-only returns/range/volatility, EMA distances/crossovers/slopes/age, completed 5/15-minute candles, prior-session OHLC/CPR and pivot distances, ATR/RSI/MACD/Bollinger features, time and nominal expiry. Bank inputs derive returns, volatility, OHLC shapes, EMA and relative volumes from original one-minute constituent candles, using dated membership and earlier verified weights only. All ten peer Indian indices and saved near-ATM monthly CE/PE paired OI, IV, volume and straddle observations are optional feature groups. No synthetic prices or network requests; unverified context is missing, not backfilled. Source-level provenance and per-split coverage are retained.

Predefine whole-session 60/20/20 chronological partitions, fit on nonoverlapping five-minute targets (every fifth completed decision), assess every eligible minute with day weighting. Compare last-close and last-close/typical-range baselines, ridge and scikit-learn histogram gradient boosting, with index-only, individual contextual groups, and combined scopes; report matched-index-only comparisons on validation. Fit imputation/scaling, feature support and prior direction probability only on training. Select once by validation OHLC error, then evaluate selected model only on later dates. Report OHLC/close MAE, direction accuracy, probability Brier against the training rate, descriptive validation-residual interval coverage and baseline-relative skill. Previously reviewed dates remain exploratory, not fresh independent confirmation. No claim of option profitability or verified live fills. No new model dependencies: custom sequence CNN/transformer is deferred until a justified improvement over the simple numeric baselines; NLP is not appropriate for numeric candles alone. No global text/macro inputs or live-order execution in this stage. The run is low CPU/disk priority; original recorder files and shared database are read only.
